A pair check, not a certification

This checks opaque sRGB text colors against WCAG 2.2 text contrast thresholds only. It does not audit a page, images, gradients, transparency, non-text controls, or overall accessibility. No APCA calculation.

Method: WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.6. External references open only when you follow a link.
